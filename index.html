CareTrip Busan

A hospital finder and wellness guide for travelers in Korea — no Korean required.

About

CareTrip Busan matches your symptoms to the right hospital department, maps nearby clinics with directions and Korean-address cards for taxis, and rounds out your trip with wellness spots around Busan. Hospital and wellness listings are drawn from the Korea Tourism Organization's public OpenAPI and Korea's national health insurance hospital directory.

Frequently asked

Do I need to create an account?

No. Every feature — symptom search, hospital map, wellness recommendations — works without signing in.

Why does the app ask for my location?

Location is used only to find hospitals and wellness spots within your chosen radius (0.5–5 km) and to calculate distance and directions. It's never collected or shared beyond that.

Which languages are supported?

Korean, English, Chinese, Japanese, German, French, Spanish, and Russian. The app switches instantly from the language menu — no restart needed.

Where does the hospital data come from?

The Korea Tourism Organization's medical tourism OpenAPI, supplemented by Korea's Health Insurance Review & Assessment Service hospital directory for broader local coverage.

Found a bug or have an idea?

Email us at the address above with your device, iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened — that's usually enough to track down the issue.
